Dino World: There was / There were

We use There was and There were to say what existed in the PAST ("vardı"). Look at the easy rule:

1️⃣ THERE WAS / WASN'T

Use for ONLY 1 thing (a/an) or Uncountable:

  • 🦖 There was a dinosaur.
  • 🍎 There was an apple.
  • 🥛 There was some milk. (Uncount.)
  • ❌ There wasn't a car.
  • ❓ Was there a tree?
2️⃣ THERE WERE / WEREN'T

Use for 2 or MORE things (Plural -s):

  • 🦕🦖 There were two dinosaurs.
  • 🌴🌴 There were many trees.
  • 🍪🍪 There were some cookies.
  • ❌ There weren't any phones.
  • ❓ Were there any birds?
