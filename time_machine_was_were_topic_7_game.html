Time Machine: WAS or WERE?

We use was and were for the PAST (yesterday, last week). Look at the easy rule:

1️⃣ WAS / WASN'T (Only 1)

Use for SINGULAR (1 person / 1 thing):

  • 🙋‍♂️ I was happy.
  • 👦 He was at school.
  • 👧 She was tired.
  • 🦖 It (1 dino) was big.
  • 🧑 Ali (1 boy) was hungry.
2️⃣ WERE / WEREN'T (2 or More + You)

Use for PLURAL (2+ people / things):

  • 🫵 You were fast! (Special!)
  • 👫 We were at the park.
  • 🦕🦖 They were huge.
  • 👦👧 Ali and Ayşe (2) were happy.
  • 🍎🍎 The apples (-s) were red.
